## Overlap handling

Do not collapse source-defined labels silently.

- Link Building is the overall acquisition strategy; Backlinks is the individual link execution/result.
- Guest Posting and Guest Blogging overlap heavily in the supplied process. Keep both labels, cross-reference them, and share the same qualification/editorial rules unless the team defines a difference.
- Forums and Forum Participation overlap heavily. Keep both labels; treat Forum Participation as ongoing engagement unless the team defines another distinction.
- Business Listing is a separate workflow from generic backlink creation.
- Ping Submission and Search Engine Submission are discovery/indexing activities, not backlink-quality activities.

## Determine execution mode

When applicable record:

- Self-publish
- Existing contributor account
- New registration
- Submission form
- Email/contact outreach
- Editor relationship
- Paid mediator/service
- Collaboration/exchange
- Search-engine/webmaster submission
- Distribution/upload
- Unknown/manual review

## Determine commercial status

Use:

- FREE
- PAID
- EXCHANGE
- RELATIONSHIP
- OWNED_ACCOUNT
- UNKNOWN

If the external site has unique editorial/publishing requirements, load [36-publisher-guidelines.md](36-publisher-guidelines.md).

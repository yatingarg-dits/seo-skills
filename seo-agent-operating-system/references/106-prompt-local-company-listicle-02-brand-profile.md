Prompt 2: Introduction and [Brand] Profile 

Using the approved research, write an SEO-friendly H1, a 100 to 150-word introduction, a 60 to 80-word methodology, and a 250 to 350-word [Brand] profile. 

For the selected brand, lead with the business priorities addressed, then industry understanding, transformation approach, capabilities, evidence, and outcomes. Use verified facts only. Do not call [Brand] “the best” without objective support. State clearly if [Brand] serves the location remotely rather than through a local office.

Local Company Listicle Workflow 

**Parameters for the complete workflow** 

- **Tone:** Neutral, evidence-led, helpful, and commercially informed. 

* **Style:** Transparent comparison based on consistent evaluation dimensions. 

- **Language:** Straightforward English without promotional superlatives or disguised ranking claims. 

* **Reader experience:** Help buyers create a shortlist based on fit, not accept a predetermined winner. 

- **Brand balance:** Give [Brand] the same evidence standard and profile structure as every other company. 

* **Research standard:** Prefer official company sources, verified location evidence, relevant case studies, and current service information. Record the access date. 

- **Quality controls:** Disclose methodology, gaps, sponsored inclusion, remote service coverage, and any limitation that affects ranking confidence. 

* **Output format:** Research table first, then methodology, profiles, buyer checklist, conclusion, FAQs, metadata, and source list. 

Apply the **Complete Writing Framework for Long-Form Content** throughout all six stages. Because the article is drafted in parts, carry forward the approved outline, methodology, sources, keyword usage, company order, and cumulative word count. Do not reset or reinterpret the rules between prompts. Maintain equal evaluation standards for [Brand] and every competitor.

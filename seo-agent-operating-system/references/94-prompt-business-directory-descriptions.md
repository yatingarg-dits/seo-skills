Business and Directory Descriptions 

**Writing parameters** 

- **Tone:** Professional, trustworthy, informative, and locally relevant where applicable. 

* **Style:** Compact third-person business description with a natural narrative, not a list of services. 

- **Language:** Simple English, active voice, and short sentences that work for global readers. 

* **Reader experience:** Readers should understand whom [Brand] helps, what changes, and why the company is relevant. 

- **Brand balance:** Approximately 70 percent buyer value and 30 percent company introduction. 

* **Quality controls:** Each version must be genuinely different, factually supportable, free from superlatives, and within its exact length limit. 

- **Output format:** Use a table showing version, description, word or character count, and keyword used. 

Create off-page descriptions for [Brand] using: 

**Target page:** [URL] 

**Topic:** [Topic] 

**Keyword:** [One keyword] 

**Industry or workflow:** [Context] 

**Market:** [Geography or global] 

Write three distinct versions: 50 to 70 words, 30 to 40 words, and no more than 200 characters including spaces. 

Begin with the audience need, explain the value offered, and mention the relevant product, service, or capability naturally. Use the keyword once. Do not keyword-stuff, claim [Brand] is “the best,” or promise that a link will increase DR, DA, rankings, or link authority. Return plain text unless the platform requires HTML.

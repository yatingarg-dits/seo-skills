# Guest Posting

## Source activity

Write and publish an article on an external relevant website with an approved contextual or author/profile reference to the client website.

## Purpose

Earn relevant editorial exposure, referral traffic, brand authority, and a qualified backlink when the publisher allows it.

## Prerequisites

Require qualified publisher from [32-backlink-qualification.md](32-backlink-qualification.md), classification from [33-backlink-activity-classification.md](33-backlink-activity-classification.md), target/anchor plan from [34-backlink-target-anchor.md](34-backlink-target-anchor.md), publisher rules from [36-publisher-guidelines.md](36-publisher-guidelines.md), and client/property context.

## Process

1. Confirm whether the publisher accepts guest contributions.
2. Identify the correct category/editor/contact/submission path.
3. Review recent published content to avoid duplicate topics.
4. Develop topic ideas that fit publisher audience and client expertise.
5. Pitch when outreach is required.
6. Record response, price/terms, and editorial conditions.
7. Create article through [35-backlink-content-generation.md](35-backlink-content-generation.md).
8. Include approved target URL/anchor only where permitted and natural.
9. Add approved author bio/signature when required.
10. Submit using [37-backlink-outreach-submission.md](37-backlink-outreach-submission.md).
11. Address publisher edits without changing client facts or link strategy without approval.
12. When live, verify through [38-backlink-verification-monitoring.md](38-backlink-verification-monitoring.md).

## Stop/review conditions

Stop when publisher requires unapproved payment, niche/audience is no longer relevant, link must be hidden/misleading/unrelated, unsupported claims are demanded, or target/anchor conflicts with guidelines.

## Output record

Store publisher, contact/submission path, topic, article reference, target URL, anchor, commercial terms, submission date, live URL, and outcome.

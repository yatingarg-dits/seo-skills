On-Site Blog Workflow 

**Parameters for the complete workflow** 

- **Tone:** Authoritative, accessible, consultative, and forward-looking. 

* **Style:** Search-led thought leadership with a connected argument, practical examples, varied section construction, and a clear conclusion. 

- **Language:** Simple professional English, active voice, concise paragraphs, and natural keywords. 

* **Reader experience:** Answer the search query early, progressively deepen understanding, and help the reader decide what to do next. 

- **Brand balance:** Education should lead. Introduce the company only where its experience or offering directly supports the reader’s decision. 

* **SEO standard:** Align H1, introduction, headings, FAQs, metadata, internal links, and CTA to one primary intent. Add secondary queries only when contextually relevant. 

- **Quality controls:** Track facts, sources, keyword frequency, repetition, word count, internal links, claims, examples, and consistency across every drafting stage. 

* **Output format:** Strategy brief, approved outline, section drafts, QA log, final article, FAQs, metadata, internal-link suggestions, and CTA. 

Apply the **Complete Writing Framework for Long-Form Content** throughout all eight stages. Carry forward every approved heading, keyword, source, tone requirement, cumulative word count, and editorial decision as the blog is written in parts. Do not repeat context from an earlier part unless the reader needs a short transition. At the end of each drafting stage, report word count, cumulative keyword frequency, new sources, and any unresolved factual gap.

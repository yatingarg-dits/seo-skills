# Backlink Community Source Database

## Purpose

Maintain a dated list of Telegram, Facebook, and Slack communities that can surface backlink, guest-post, publisher, outreach, or relationship opportunities.

These communities are **discovery sources only**. A community mention, shared sheet, seller claim, or member recommendation never makes a website approved.

For every discovered domain, route through:

`44 historical check -> 32 qualification -> 42 commercial/access -> 33 activity -> 34 target/anchor -> execution/verification`

## Research status

- Last web research: `2026-09-17`
- Scope: English/global SEO, guest posting, link building, publisher collaboration, and digital-marketing communities
- Member counts are intentionally not stored because they change frequently.
- Revalidate access, group rules, fees, activity level, and channel names before relying on a community.

## Source roles

Use one primary role:

- `DIRECT_OPPORTUNITY` - guest posts, publisher requests, or link opportunities are explicitly shared.
- `RELATIONSHIP_NETWORKING` - build relationships and identify publishers/partners; do not assume solicitation is allowed.
- `MARKETPLACE_HIGH_RISK` - open buying/selling/exchange environment; discovery only and mandatory manual review.
- `RESEARCH_ONLY` - useful for tactics, market intelligence, or risk monitoring; do not source placements automatically.

## Verification statuses

- `PUBLIC_PAGE_VERIFIED` - a public landing/group/channel page was visible during research.
- `DISCOVERED_NEEDS_JOIN_VERIFY` - public references exist, but current internal activity/rules require joining to confirm.
- `RESEARCH_ONLY_HIGH_RISK` - public source exists but its observed behavior is too risky for automatic opportunity intake.

## Intake rules

1. Store the `source_id` for every candidate discovered from this database.
2. Capture the exact post/message URL when technically available and permitted.
3. Never copy a community's claimed DA/DR/traffic into approved metrics without independent verification.
4. Never infer that a site is free because a group title says "free guest posts".
5. Apply [42-backlink-commercial-classification.md](42-backlink-commercial-classification.md) after direct publisher verification.
6. Apply [44-historical-backlink-database.md](44-historical-backlink-database.md) before researching a domain again.
7. Use `MARKETPLACE_HIGH_RISK` sources only with manual review before outreach/payment/exchange.
8. Use `RESEARCH_ONLY` sources for intelligence, not placement execution.
9. Revalidate community access/rules at least when the recorded source becomes stale or before a new campaign depends on it.
10. Record source-quality feedback through [39-backlink-feedback-learning.md](39-backlink-feedback-learning.md).

## Source-quality learning

Track by `source_id`:

- Candidates reviewed
- Qualified candidates
- Rejected candidates
- Paid-only candidates
- Live placements
- Lost links
- Spam/risk incidents
- Average time to useful opportunity
- Human source rating

Do not rank a community by volume alone. Prefer sources that repeatedly produce relevant, legitimate, verifiable publishers for the target client/property.

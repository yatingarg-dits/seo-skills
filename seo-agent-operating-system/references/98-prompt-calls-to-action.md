Calls to Action 

**Writing parameters** 

- **Tone:** Consultative, reassuring, action-oriented, and low pressure. 

* **Style:** Outcome-led microcopy based on the visitor’s stage of decision-making. 

- **Language:** Clear verbs, concrete value, and no vague phrases such as “Learn More” when a specific action is possible. 

* **Reader experience:** The next step should feel useful, relevant, and easy to understand. 

- **Brand balance:** Focus on what the visitor receives rather than what [Brand] wants. 

* **Quality controls:** Each option must represent a different intent, avoid promises, and remain consistent with the page offer. 

- **Output format:** Table with funnel stage, title, description, button text, and recommended placement. 

Create 10 CTAs for: 

**Topic:** [Topic] 

**Keyword:** [Keyword] 

**Business priority:** [Priority] 

**Audience:** [Audience] 

**Conversion:** [Consultation / assessment / form / demo] 

For each, provide a question-style title of no more than 10 words, a 15 to 22-word description, and a 2 to 5-word button label. Vary the intent across discovery, assessment, planning, validation, and execution. Avoid false urgency and repeated “fill out the form” language. Prefer consultative language such as “Discuss Your Business Priority.”

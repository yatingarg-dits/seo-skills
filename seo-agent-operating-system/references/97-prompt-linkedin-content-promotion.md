LinkedIn Content Promotion 

**Writing parameters** 

- **Tone:** Conversational, insightful, confident, and executive-friendly. 

* **Style:** Strong opening, short paragraphs, varied sentence rhythm, one meaningful takeaway, and one natural CTA. 

- **Language:** Human, simple, and professional. Avoid corporate filler, clickbait, exaggerated suspense, and generic motivational statements. 

* **Reader experience:** The reader should recognize a real business tension, gain a useful perspective, and feel curious about the full content. 

- **Brand balance:** Insight first, promotion second. Keep direct brand promotion below 25 percent. 

* **Quality controls:** Do not disclose confidential case-study details, invent first-person experiences, or use unsupported claims. 

- **Output format:** Provide the final post, CTA line, recommended link placement, and hashtags in separate blocks. 

Create a LinkedIn post using: 

**Content title:** [Title] 

**Business question:** [Question] 

**Audience:** [Decision-makers] 

**Industry:** [Industry] 

**Link:** [URL] 

**Desired action:** [Read / discuss / meet] 

Write 120 to 180 words in a conversational executive B2B tone. Open with a recognizable business tension. Share enough insight to establish relevance without revealing the whole article. Present technology as an enabler of broader change. Close with one clear CTA. 

Add 3 to 5 specific hashtags. If trending hashtags are requested, research current usage first. Do not use engagement bait, fake personal experience, exaggerated curiosity gaps, or unsupported outcomes.

# Content Research and Source Reliability

## Purpose

Use [07-seo-content-tool-stack.md](07-seo-content-tool-stack.md) for approved research/content tools. ChatGPT or another AI tool may help discover/synthesize sources, but the AI output itself is not source evidence.

Create an auditable research pack for factual content and classify evidence by source reliability.


## Contents

- Research sequence and rules
- Source reliability tiers
- Research-pack fields
- Claim handling
- Handoff
## Research sequence

Follow:

`Question -> Search -> Source -> Extract -> Cross-check -> Synthesize -> Cite -> Confidence`

## Research rules

1. Prefer primary sources where available.
2. Separate fact from opinion.
3. Record source URLs/references.
4. Record publication/update date when relevant.
5. Check freshness when the fact can change.
6. Cross-check important claims.
7. Do not convert assumptions into facts.
8. Do not fabricate statistics or quotations.
9. Preserve the original context around statistics.
10. Flag conflicting evidence.
11. Flag insufficient evidence.
12. Identify source type/tier.
13. Maintain traceability from claim to source.
14. Escalate sensitive/high-risk claims.

## Source reliability tiers

### Tier A - Primary

Examples:

- Government
- Regulator
- Standards organization
- Official company documentation/data
- Original research

Prefer for material factual claims when available.

### Tier B - Strong Secondary

Examples:

- Respected research institutions
- Established publications
- Credible industry organizations

### Tier C - Industry/Expert

Examples:

- Specialist publications
- Credible practitioners
- Reputable company research

Use with context and disclose perspective/limitations where relevant.

### Tier D - Community

Examples:

- Forums
- Reddit
- Social discussion
- User-generated material

Use for sentiment, experience, examples, and community perspective. Do not automatically treat as authoritative evidence.

### Tier E - Unverified

Examples:

- Aggregators
- Scraped pages
- Anonymous claims
- Low-quality AI-generated material

Do not support material factual claims solely with Tier E.

## Research-pack fields

For each useful source record:

- Source title
- URL/reference
- Publisher/owner
- Source tier
- Published/updated date when available
- Accessed/checked date
- Claim/topic supported
- Extracted fact/insight
- Conflicting source if any
- Confidence
- Notes/limitations

## Claim handling

Use:

- SUPPORTED
- SUPPORTED_WITH_LIMITATION
- CONFLICTING_EVIDENCE
- INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE
- OUTDATED_SOURCE
- UNSUPPORTED
- NEEDS_EXPERT_REVIEW

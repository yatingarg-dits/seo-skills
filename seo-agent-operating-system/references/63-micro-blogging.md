# Micro Blogging

## Source activity

Share short-form updates, promotions, or content snippets on approved micro-blogging/social publishing platforms.

## Purpose

Use [07-seo-content-tool-stack.md](07-seo-content-tool-stack.md) for Buffer scheduling/approval/analytics and Canva visual production when those tools are approved.

Use short-form distribution to increase content visibility, referral opportunities, brand presence, and relevant engagement. Do not promise direct ranking improvement from social engagement alone.

## Prerequisites

Load [03-client-context.md](03-client-context.md) and [04-property-context.md](04-property-context.md).

Confirm approved platform/account, target URL if required, brand voice, topic/campaign, and approved media/hashtags when applicable.

## Process

1. Select a platform relevant to the client's audience.
2. Confirm account access and publishing permission.
3. Choose the page/content/campaign to promote.
4. Draft a short platform-native post.
5. Use a clear value-led hook rather than link-only spam.
6. Add the approved URL only where useful and allowed.
7. Add relevant hashtags/tags according to platform/client rules.
8. Add approved image/video if it improves the post.
9. Preview and publish.
10. Store the post URL and publication date.
11. Track referral traffic/engagement only when part of the property KPI set.

## Quality checks

Ensure the post is relevant, link matches the message, claims are supported, promotional copy is not excessively repeated, and platform rules are followed.

## Stop/review conditions

Require human review when brand, legal, offer, pricing, or campaign approval is missing.

## Output record

Store platform, account, post URL, topic, target URL, copy/media reference, publish date, tracked metrics, status, and failure reason.

# Business Listing

## Source activity

List the business on relevant online directories and keep business information consistent across listings.

## Purpose

Create, claim, correct, verify, and maintain local/niche business listings. Treat this as a separate activity from generic backlink building.

## Detailed modules

Use:

- [40-business-listing-discovery.md](40-business-listing-discovery.md) for discovery and qualification.
- [41-business-listing-execution.md](41-business-listing-execution.md) for creation, claim, correction, verification, and maintenance.

## Prerequisites

Load [04-property-context.md](04-property-context.md) and confirm approved business name, address/service area, phone, website, categories, description, hours, approved email, logo/images, social profiles, and services/products.

Do not invent missing business data.

## Process

1. Find relevant local, national, niche, map, association, and directory platforms.
2. Check whether the business already has a listing.
3. Classify the listing as new, correct, incorrect, duplicate, claim-required, paid-only, or not relevant.
4. Qualify the platform for market, niche, legitimacy, control/update ability, and verification requirements.
5. Prepare platform-required business data using property-approved values.
6. Create, claim, correct, or merge according to [41-business-listing-execution.md](41-business-listing-execution.md).
7. Complete platform verification where required.
8. Verify public listing, website link, category, business/NAP details, and duplicate status.
9. Store public URL and account/credential reference.
10. Monitor incorrect edits, duplicates, broken links, and platform changes.

## Stop/review conditions

Use `MISSING_PROPERTY_CONTEXT` if required business data is missing.

Use manual review when paid approval is required, ownership/claim conflict exists, a duplicate cannot be safely merged, verification is unsupported, or existing data conflicts with approved property data.

## Output record

Store platform/domain, submission/public URL, existing listing status, market/niche, free/paid, business-data version, verification method/status, listing status, dates, and failure/duplicate reason.

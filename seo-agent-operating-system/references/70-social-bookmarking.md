# Social Bookmarking

## Source activity

Share useful client pages/content on approved social-bookmarking or community-discovery platforms.

## Purpose

Increase content discovery, referral traffic, and community visibility. Treat any backlink as a secondary outcome and verify its attributes/status.

## Prerequisites

Require approved platform/account, relevant page/content, title, description, tags/category, and community/platform rules.

## Process

1. Select a legitimate bookmarking/community platform relevant to the content.
2. Check whether the same URL was already submitted by the client account.
3. Confirm the page provides genuine value to the platform/community.
4. Write an accurate, appealing title and description.
5. Choose relevant category/tags.
6. Submit the canonical client URL.
7. Follow community rules; do not mass-submit every client URL.
8. Verify the public bookmark/post page.
9. Record referral/link status when useful.
10. Monitor removal/moderation outcomes.

## Stop/reject conditions

Reject if the platform is inactive/spammy, community prohibits promotional submissions, URL is irrelevant, or submission would duplicate prior spam-like activity.

## Handoff

If the bookmark creates a live link, use [38-backlink-verification-monitoring.md](38-backlink-verification-monitoring.md).

## Output record

Store platform, account, source URL, bookmark/post URL, category/tags, submission date, moderation status, backlink status, and failure reason.

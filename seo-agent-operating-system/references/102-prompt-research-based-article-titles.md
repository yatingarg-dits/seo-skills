Research-Based Article Titles 

**Writing parameters** 

- **Tone:** Intelligent, specific, current, and useful. 

* **Style:** Search-aware editorial titles based on genuine business questions and decision intent. 

- **Language:** Natural phrasing without sensationalism, filler words, or repetitive formulas. 

* **Reader experience:** Each title should signal a clear benefit, question, risk, or decision. 

- **Brand balance:** Avoid inserting [Brand] into titles unless the content is explicitly brand-led. 

* **Quality controls:** Remove duplicate angles, unsupported claims, forced keywords, vague words such as “revolutionize,” and titles disconnected from available evidence. 

- **Output format:** Table with title, search intent, audience stage, content angle, primary keyword, and character count. 

Research and propose 15 titles using: 

**Industry:** [Industry] 

**Business priorities:** [Priorities] 

**Audience:** [Startups / mid-market / enterprises] 

**Relevant capabilities or services:** [Capabilities or services] 

**Geography:** [If relevant] 

Create a mix of how, why, what, decision, comparison, risk, implementation, and outcome-led titles. Target 50 to 70 characters. Lead with an industry problem or outcome, with technology as the enabler. Group suggestions by search intent and add a one-line rationale for each group.

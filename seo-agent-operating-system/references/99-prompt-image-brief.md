Image Brief 

**Creative parameters** 

- **Tone:** Credible, progressive, human, and business-focused. 

* **Visual style:** Premium editorial technology aesthetic with clean composition, realistic people or environments, subtle data cues, and restrained branding. 

- **Viewer experience:** The central business message should be understandable within three seconds. 

* **Brand balance:** Business context must dominate. Technology should support the story rather than become decorative spectacle. 

- **Accessibility:** Maintain readable contrast, avoid dense details, reserve safe space, and do not rely on colour alone to communicate meaning. 

* **Quality controls:** Verify geography, industry details, cultural representation, medical accuracy, brand colours, aspect ratio, and absence of unwanted text or logos. 

- **Output format:** Provide concept, visual hierarchy, composition, palette, lighting, inclusions, exclusions, accessibility notes, and final generation prompt. 

Create a production-ready image brief using: 

**Title:** [Title] 

**Business message:** [Change and value] 

**Industry or workflow:** [Context] 

**Size:** [Width × height] 

**Brand style:** Clean, credible, modern, global, business-first, and aligned with [Brand] colours 

Show the business environment, people, workflow, or connected system before the technology. Use AI, cloud, or data elements only when they clarify the transformation. Keep interfaces abstract unless approved screenshots are provided. Use flags only when geography is central. 

Avoid generic robots, oversized AI brains, fake dashboards, clutter, stereotypes, unlicensed logos, and misleading medical or technical visuals. Leave safe space for the title when needed. 

Return the visual concept, composition, colour direction, elements to include, elements to avoid, and final image-generation prompt.

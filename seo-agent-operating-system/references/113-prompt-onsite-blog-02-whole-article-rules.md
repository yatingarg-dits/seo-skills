Prompt 2: Whole-Article Rules 

Apply these rules throughout: lead with the business decision; maintain one argument; use keywords naturally; track total keyword use; cite current reliable sources; never invent facts; use one H1 and logical H2/H3s; avoid em dashes, emojis, clichés, and repetition; keep promotion proportionate; end with a business-focused next step. 

Confirm the plan, total length, section allocation, keyword range, and research needs before drafting.

# Backlink Research and Execution Benchmarks

## Purpose

Provide an experienced-operator capacity benchmark by activity instead of one generic "backlinks per day" target.

Quality and policy compliance always override quantity.

## Status of these numbers

These are `PROVISIONAL_PLATFORM_DEFAULT_V1` capacity ranges for planning and agent testing. They are not client promises or ranking KPIs.

Agency/client/property overrides may replace them after real production data is collected.

## Daily capacity reference per experienced operator

| Activity | Provisional daily capacity | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Candidate discovery from prepared lists/feeds | 50-100 domains | Deduplicate before counting |
| Manual Google/competitor discovery | 25-50 domains | Depends on niche and search depth |
| Full website qualification | 30-50 domains | Includes manual quality/risk review |
| Personalized outreach | 20-40 contacts | Exclude mass templated spam |
| Low-friction self-publish/profile/bookmark tasks | 20-40 completed | Only qualified relevant platforms |
| Medium-friction registrations/submissions | 10-20 completed | Verification/CAPTCHA/profile setup reduces volume |
| Business listings/citations | 10-20 completed | New listings; NAP/verification quality required |
| Forum/Q&A/community contributions | 5-15 useful contributions | Do not post link-only answers |
| Long-form article/Web 2.0 submissions | 1-3 completed | Requires original useful content |
| Guest-post submissions ready for editor review | 1-3 completed | Excludes publisher response delay |
| Manual live-link verification | 20-40 URLs | Faster with tooling |
| Tool-assisted link-status verification | 50-100+ URLs | Human review still required for anomalies |

## Benchmark interpretation

Do not measure success only by completed link count.

Track separately:

- Candidates found
- Candidates qualified
- Opportunities approved
- Outreach sent
- Responses
- Submissions completed
- Links published
- Links verified/indexed where relevant
- Rejections by reason
- Paid/free/exchange mix
- Unique referring domains
- Target-page distribution
- Human quality score

## Quality gates

A day with fewer high-quality editorial placements can outperform a day with many low-value profile/comment links.

Do not force an operator/agent to hit volume by:

- lowering qualification thresholds
- using irrelevant sites
- repeating the same referring domain unnecessarily
- using spammy automation
- posting thin/duplicate content
- violating platform/publisher rules

## Calibration

After at least 4 weeks of tracked production, calculate actual capacity by activity and update the agency override using:

- Median completed/day
- P75 completed/day
- Acceptance rate
- Publication rate
- Rework rate
- Link survival rate
- Average human review score

Replace provisional numbers only through approved [02-agency-seo-overrides.md](02-agency-seo-overrides.md).

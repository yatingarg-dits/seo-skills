# Podcasts

## Source activity

Create a client podcast episode or participate as a guest on a relevant external podcast and connect listeners to approved client resources.

## Purpose

Build brand/expert visibility, audience engagement, referral traffic, and editorial mentions/links in show notes where appropriate.

## Prerequisites

Require approved speaker/host identity, topic/expertise area, bio, website/landing page, talking-point boundaries, and external show qualification for guest appearances.

## Process - guest podcast

1. Find podcasts relevant to the client's niche/audience.
2. Review recent episodes, guests, quality, audience fit, and contact route.
3. Qualify the publisher/show before outreach.
4. Pitch a useful topic/expertise angle, not only a backlink request.
5. Confirm recording format, date, topic, bio, and show-note requirements.
6. Prepare approved talking points and links/resources.
7. Participate in recording.
8. When published, verify episode/show-note page and website reference.
9. Record live URL and link status.

## Process - own podcast

1. Define episode objective and topic.
2. Prepare outline/script and approved participants.
3. Record/edit using approved assets.
4. Publish to approved hosting/distribution channels.
5. Add accurate show notes and relevant client links.
6. Verify live episode pages.

## Output record

Store show/platform, episode title, guest/host, date, episode URL, show-note target URL, link status, and tracked referral/engagement metrics.

# Video Submission

## Source activity

Publish relevant video content on approved video platforms and connect it to the client website where platform rules allow.

## Purpose

Use [07-seo-content-tool-stack.md](07-seo-content-tool-stack.md) for approved visual/content creation and social publishing tools where relevant.

Use video publishing to increase brand/content discovery, referral traffic, audience engagement, and searchable multimedia presence.

## Prerequisites

Confirm approved video file, title/topic, description, thumbnail, target URL, channel/account, and rights/permissions for visuals, music, voice, and footage.

## Process

1. Choose an approved platform relevant to the audience.
2. Confirm channel/account access.
3. Match video topic to a client page, campaign, product, service, or educational resource.
4. Prepare a descriptive title aligned with the actual video.
5. Write a useful description.
6. Add the approved website URL where the platform permits it.
7. Add categories/tags/chapters/captions when applicable.
8. Upload thumbnail and accessibility elements when available.
9. Set visibility/publish schedule according to campaign rules.
10. Publish and verify the live video page.
11. Verify the website link if present.
12. Store the video URL and publication details.

## Quality checks

Video must be relevant and watchable, title/description must not be misleading, link must point to a relevant live page, unauthorized media must not be used, and platform rules must be followed.

## Handoff

If the video page creates a backlink, record it through [38-backlink-verification-monitoring.md](38-backlink-verification-monitoring.md).

## Output record

Store platform, channel, video URL, target URL, title, publish date, link status, and tracked performance metrics.

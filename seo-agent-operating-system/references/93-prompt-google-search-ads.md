Google Search Ads 

**Writing parameters** 

- **Tone:** Confident, concise, credible, and consultative. 

* **Style:** Direct-response B2B copy built around buyer intent, business value, and a clear action. 

- **Language:** Plain English, active voice, short phrases, and no jargon unless the audience uses it. 

* **Reader experience:** The buyer should immediately understand relevance, value, and the next step. 

- **Brand balance:** Lead with the buyer’s need. Use [Brand] as the credible transformation partner, not the hero of every line. 

* **Quality controls:** Every asset must be distinct, accurate, grammatically complete, and compliant with Google Ads limits and policies. 

- **Output format:** Use separate tables for headlines, descriptions, callouts, snippets, and sitelinks. Include character counts and keyword intent. 

Act as a senior B2B performance marketing strategist. Create Google Search Ad assets using: 

**Brand:** [Brand] 

**Business priority:** [Buyer goal] 

**Audience:** [Roles, industries, and company stage] 

**Geography:** [Market] 

**Relevant capability or service:** [Approved capability or service] 

**Offer:** [Consultation, assessment, workshop, or demo] 

**Verified proof:** [Evidence or none] 

**Landing page:** [URL] 

**Keywords:** [Keywords] 

**Goal:** Qualified business enquiries 

Create 15 unique headlines of no more than 30 characters, 4 descriptions of no more than 90 characters, 5 callouts, 3 valid structured snippet groups, and 4 sitelinks with two descriptions each. 

Prioritize the buyer’s problem, transformation value, and credible differentiation. Use keywords naturally. Do not invent discounts, urgency, statistics, guarantees, or proof. Include a clear next step such as “Discuss Your Priority” where suitable. Show exact character counts.

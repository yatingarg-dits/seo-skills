Core Direction for Every Content Prompt 

Replace all text in square brackets before use. Adapt the content journey to the format and audience. For business content, use this flexible order where relevant: 

1. Business priority or operational problem 

2) Industry, workflow, user, and system context 

3. What must change, improve, or be decided and why 

4) The approach, service, product, technology, or capability that enables progress 

5. Verified evidence 

6) Measurable or observable business outcomes 

7. A consultative next step 

Define the brand positioning before writing. Present the brand according to its approved business model, audience, market, category, and value proposition. Do not reduce a brand to a list of services, products, or capabilities. 

Use only the capabilities, services, products, industries, proof points, and terminology supplied for the selected brand. Do not transfer information from another company or project. 

Write in simple, professional English. Do not use em dashes, emojis, inflated claims, invented facts, fake quotations, keyword stuffing, or language intended to manipulate AI detectors. Do not imitate or claim employment at Forbes or another publication. Research time-sensitive information and cite reliable primary sources.

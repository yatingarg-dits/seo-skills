# Article Submission

## Source activity

Write and submit informative articles to approved third-party article directories or publishing platforms.

## Purpose

Distribute useful expertise and earn referral visibility/link exposure where the destination is legitimate. Do not mass-submit duplicated/spun articles.

## Prerequisites

Require qualified publishing platform, topic relevant to client and platform, original approved article, target URL/anchor plan when links are allowed, and author/profile data.

## Process

1. Identify a relevant article-submission/publishing platform.
2. Qualify the site using [32-backlink-qualification.md](32-backlink-qualification.md).
3. Read editorial, duplication, author, and link rules.
4. Check whether the topic already exists on the platform.
5. Create original content through [35-backlink-content-generation.md](35-backlink-content-generation.md) when appropriate.
6. Add only permitted, relevant links.
7. Complete author bio/profile if required.
8. Submit/publish.
9. Handle editorial feedback.
10. Verify live page and backlink through [38-backlink-verification-monitoring.md](38-backlink-verification-monitoring.md).

## Stop/reject conditions

Reject if the platform exists mainly for low-quality mass submissions, requires duplicated/spun content, paid terms lack approval, or link/content rules conflict with client requirements.

## Output record

Store platform, article title, submission/live URL, author, target URL, anchor, submission date, live date, and outcome.

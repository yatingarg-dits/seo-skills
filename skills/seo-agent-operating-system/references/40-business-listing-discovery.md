# Business Listing Discovery and Qualification

## Purpose

Discover and qualify business listing/citation platforms as a separate workflow from backlink research.

A listing may create a link, but its data requirements and execution logic are different from guest posts or editorial backlinks.

## Prerequisites

Load:

- [01-platform-seo-context.md](01-platform-seo-context.md)
- [04-property-context.md](04-property-context.md)

## Discovery sources

Use approved sources such as:

- Search engines
- Existing agency listing database
- Competitor citation research
- Local/niche directories
- Industry associations
- Map/local ecosystems
- Historical client listing sheets
- Approved API/data providers

Record source and date found.

## Qualification checks

Evaluate:

- Listing platform is live
- Relevant country/market
- Relevant niche/category
- Business type accepted
- Free/paid status
- Verification requirement
- Account requirement
- Duplicate listing risk
- Ability to control/update profile
- Listing page indexability when relevant
- Platform legitimacy/quality
- Existing property listing

Do not approve a listing solely because it has high DA or creates a link.

## Existing-listing check

Search for:

- Exact business name
- Name variants
- Address variants
- Phone variants
- Domain

Classify:

- NOT_LISTED
- LISTED_CORRECT
- LISTED_INCORRECT
- DUPLICATE_LISTING
- CLAIM_REQUIRED
- PAID_ONLY
- NOT_RELEVANT
- MANUAL_REVIEW

## Required listing fields

Determine what the platform requires, for example:

- Business name
- Category
- Description
- Address/service area
- Phone
- Website
- Email
- Hours
- Logo/images
- Social profiles
- Services/products
- Verification method

Do not invent missing client details.

## Output

Return:

- Platform/domain
- Listing URL or submission URL
- Source
- Market/niche
- Free/paid
- Existing-listing status
- Verification method
- Required fields
- Qualification decision
- Reason
